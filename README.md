# منوی QR رستورانت | QR Restaurant Menu

A QR-code menu system for restaurants and cafés. Each table gets its own printed QR card — the customer scans it and sees the menu on their phone, no app needed. The owner manages dishes, categories and prices from a simple admin panel, and orders can go straight to WhatsApp.

## How it works | طرز کار
1. The owner adds categories and dishes (name, price in AFN, photo, available/unavailable)
2. The system makes one QR card per table — print and put on the table
3. The customer scans the QR → the menu opens on their phone
4. The customer picks dishes and sends the order on WhatsApp

## Features | امکانات
- Admin panel: categories, dishes, prices, photos, availability toggle
- Customer menu: categories, order basket, WhatsApp order button
- One QR card per table (printable PNG)
- Settings: restaurant name, phone/WhatsApp, admin password
- Works on the customer's own mobile data — no restaurant Wi-Fi required
- The QR link is permanent — print once, use forever

## Live demo
